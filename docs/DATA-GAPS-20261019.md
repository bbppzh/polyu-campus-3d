## Used in this pass

- **Hotel ICON entrances (registered, geometry acceptance passed)**: the G/F
  image is registered to the full compound using six control points and three
  independent check points. Each entrance uses its own component's exposed
  wall. All 24 door/canopy bottom corners and all 96 entrance-assembly bottom
  corners were checked against exported structural triangles and neighbouring
  context. Three pedestrian-height before/after renders were reviewed against
  G/F locations. Hotel signage uses the same registered hotel entrance.
  See [registration, geometry checks and render comparisons](HOTEL-ENTRANCES-20261019.md).
  These are model-placement checks; dimensions and vertical datum remain
  estimates, and school/staff views are not matched real-world photographs.
- **LKS app materials**: the final GLB's `LKS_terracotta`, `LKS_glazing`,
  `LKS_stone` and `LKS_metal` are now mapped to the app's brick/glass/stone
  shaders instead of falling back to untouched Blender materials.
- **Block Z** already carries the two wings, low teaching block and sky link in
  the final builder; courtyard references are now preserved locally.
